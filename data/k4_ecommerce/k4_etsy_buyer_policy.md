# Etsy Buyer Policy (starter summary)

This policy is part of Etsy's Terms of Use and governs what is expected of, and available to, buyers shopping on Etsy. It is a companion document to the separate Seller Policy.

## Key content

**1. Etsy's Limitations as a Marketplace**
- Buyers purchase from individual sellers, not from Etsy directly; Etsy does not pre-screen or endorse items/content; each seller sets their own processing times, shipping methods, and shop policies.
- Buyers can flag items/shops that violate Etsy policy; flagging is confidential.

**2. Communicating with Other Members**
- Messages may not be used for spam, harassment, interference (e.g., warning buyers away from a seller, click fraud, negative-review purchases), or facilitating off-platform transactions (sharing contact/payment info, QR codes, etc.).
- Buyers should avoid sharing personal information beyond what's needed to complete a transaction.

**3. Purchasing an Item**
- Buyers must read item descriptions/shop policies, submit appropriate payment, and provide accurate shipping info.
- Supported payment methods include major cards, Etsy Gift Cards/Credits/Coupons, bank transfer services, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Klarna (subject to each provider's own terms).
- All transactions must go through Etsy checkout to be eligible for the Case System (excludes Standalone PayPal orders); sharing contact info/QR codes for off-platform payment is prohibited.

**4. Leaving a Review**
- Buyers can leave a 1–5 star review with photo/video within 100 days of delivery (or 100 days from digital download / 12 months from purchase, whichever is first); reviews can be edited during that window.
- Reviews may not contain graphic/obscene content, be about things outside the seller's control (e.g., carrier or Etsy issues), include shilling, or undermine the review system's integrity.

**5. Creating and Uploading Content**
- Same content restrictions as sellers: no hate speech, threats/harassment, IP violations, false/misleading content, spam, private info, off-platform transaction facilitation, prohibited medical claims, or sexualization of minors.

**6. Reporting Problems / Returns**
- Buyers must first contact the seller via "Help with Order"; sellers get 48 hours to resolve before a case can be opened.
- Etsy's Purchase Protection Program covers items that never arrive, arrive late/damaged, or don't match the listing — with detailed eligibility criteria (including DDP-related customs charge disputes for US buyers) and listed exclusions (e.g., altered/used items, in-person purchases, non-Etsy-Payments transactions).
- On-platform refunds are only available within 180 days of the transaction; only sellers can cancel orders, buyers can request cancellation via Messages.
- EU/UK buyers have a 14-day "cooling off"/right of withdrawal for returns, with some exceptions (custom/digital items vary).
