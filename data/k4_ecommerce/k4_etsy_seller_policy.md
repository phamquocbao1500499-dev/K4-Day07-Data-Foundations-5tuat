# Etsy Seller Policy (starter summary)

This policy is part of Etsy's Terms of Use and governs what is expected of sellers who open a shop on Etsy. It is a companion document to the separate Buyer Policy.

## Key content

**1. Selling Basics**
- Items must be made, designed, handpicked, or sourced by the seller (per Etsy's Creativity Standards); sellers must disclose how items are made, by whom, shop members involved, production partners, and any AI-generated content, and must use their own photos/videos.
- Prohibited items, services, and IP-infringing goods cannot be sold; listings must offer an actual item for sale (no referral codes, want ads, or crowdfunding-only listings); dropshipping/reselling is generally not allowed.
- Sellers must give honest shop/listing info, honor shop policies, avoid off-platform transactions, avoid duplicate shops/search manipulation, avoid price coordination with other sellers, and use Delivery Duty Paid (DDP) shipping for US-bound orders.
- Sellers are responsible for complying with all applicable laws/labeling requirements for their products.
- Seller Service Level Standards: honor shipping/processing times, respond to messages promptly, honor shop policy commitments, resolve order issues, notify/cancel when unable to fulfill, and be honest in pricing/promotions.
- Selling fees apply for listing, selling, advertising, and certain features.

**2. Community Membership**
- Content rules ban hateful/derogatory content, threats/harassment/extortion, IP violations, false/misleading content, spam, private info sharing, off-platform transaction facilitation, prohibited medical claims, and sexualization of minors.
- Sellers who process buyer personal data (e.g., via Etsy API) may be independent data controllers under GDPR and must honor data subject rights and indemnify Etsy for related claims.
- Messages may not be used for spam, harassment, interference (e.g., warning buyers away from competitors, click fraud, fake negative reviews), or off-platform deal-making.
- EU/UK buyers have a 14-day right of withdrawal that sellers must accommodate (with exceptions like custom/perishable items).

**3. Feedback, Cases, and Success**
- Buyers can leave reviews (1–5 stars, photo/video) within 100 days of delivery/download; reviews may not be shilled, off-topic, or abusive.
- Etsy's Case System: buyers must contact sellers first; sellers get 48 hours to resolve before a case can be opened; Etsy may step in to resolve, including refunds.
- Purchase Protection Program for Sellers covers certain cases; outside those, sellers generally must refund (including shipping), and Etsy may recoup funds from the seller's account.
- Etsy may limit shop/listing visibility, restrict payment accounts, or suspend/terminate accounts for policy violations, unusual activity, or standards failures.
